One original 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired, written for the [The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

```
Come in, then. Mind the third step — the mason who built it took the repair to his grave, and I have never forgiven him. Thirty years I have held this mountain, and still the kettle sings before the scouts do. You came for the crown, I suppose. Everyone does. It sits on its cushion, unpolished since your grandfather's visit. Sit. The chair opposite is the comfortable one; I keep it for auditors. Heroes always arrive at supper, swords drawn, speeches rehearsed, and I am always mid-sentence in a letter about grain. Strike, then. But first — pass the salt.
```

`main.py` (standard library only) writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
